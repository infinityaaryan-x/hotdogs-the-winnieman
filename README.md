# Hotdogs The Winnieman

Create a polished, high-conversion bilingual (English + Spanish) website for “Hotdogs the Winnieman”, a street-style hotdog business in Van Nuys, California. Business facts: address 15244 Vanowen St, Van Nuys, CA 91405, USA; stated schedule Tuesday–Saturday, 5:00 PM–11:00 PM; Instagram @hotdog_the_winnieman_22 (https://www.instagram.com/hotdog_the_winnieman_22/). Food photos on Instagram show loaded hotdogs/sausages with ketchup, mustard, mayo, jalapeños and toppings. Use a bold premium urban street-food art direction: near-black charcoal, warm cream typography, vivid tomato red and mustard yellow accents, subtle neon glow, editorial typography, tactile grain, excellent spacing, mobile-first. Make a striking hero with dramatic close-up loaded hotdog photography from high-quality royalty-free image assets; never invent business photography or show Instagram screenshots. Hero headline “BIG FLAVOR. STREET SOUL.” plus Spanish translation and CTAs “Explore the menu” / “Find us tonight”. Implement a working EN/ES language toggle that switches all major copy. Include sticky nav, food/menu section using descriptive categories only (do not invent specific product names or prices; say “Ask in person / Pregunta en persona”), food gallery, brand/about section, location with Google Maps directions link, hours, Instagram link, and mobile sticky directions CTA. Do not invent phone number, delivery, catering, prices, exact menu names, or unsupported claims. Add subtle motion, hover states, accessible contrast, semantic HTML, SEO metadata. Distinctive design, not a generic restaurant template. Create preview only; do not publish/deploy.
